In chats with businesses, WinZapp shows the catalog, builds the cart and tracks orders.

Catalog and cart

In "Business catalog and cart" (the chat's context menu, or Alt+Shift+B), the list shows the products with price and availability. From there you use "Add to cart", "Change quantity", "Remove from cart" and "Empty cart". "Send order" sends the order only once, after a question that says what is going and to whom. If the business turned the cart off, the catalog can still be browsed and WinZapp says it does not accept orders there. A product the business sends from its catalog appears with name, price and description.

Orders

Order messages and order updates appear in the message list, in the chat preview and in notifications, with items, total and state. Enter on the order message (or "View order" in the message menu) opens the details.

WhatsApp Business accounts

Only on WhatsApp Business accounts: in the orders window, "Accept order", "Decline order" and "update status" for the orders received; in the chat menu, the "Business tools" submenu, with "Create an order for this contact" (Ctrl+Shift+N) and "Accept carts in my catalog". Where they do not apply, WinZapp says why.

Orders created by the seller

Enter on an order a seller created for you opens its details, with items and subtotal. When the order is pending and is paid outside the app, "Confirm order info" sends the seller the confirmation, like WhatsApp Web; the dialog says which buyer responses WinZapp does not offer yet.
